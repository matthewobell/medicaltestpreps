Delete Your Medical Test Prep Account

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Medical Test Prep lets you request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. In the app, go to Settings → Delete my data → Request account deletion, or
  2. Email admin@medicaltestprep.org from the email address you used to sign up, with the subject “Account deletion request – Medical Test Prep”.

We will confirm your request and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and login
  • Your name, email address, institution, and graduation year
  • Your Premium status
  • Your study-time records
  • Flashcards and questions you created that are stored online

What is kept

  • Anonymous, aggregated usage statistics that cannot be linked back to you

Data stored on your device

Quiz history stored on your phone can be removed at any time in the app under Settings → Delete my data, or by uninstalling the app.

Questions

Contact us at admin@medicaltestprep.org. For more information, see our Privacy Policy.
