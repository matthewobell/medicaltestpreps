We’re Medical Test Prep!

Medical Test Prep is an independent study tool designed to help surgical residents, fellows, and practicing surgeons prepare for the EMIGS and FLS certification examinations.

Our question bank is built from publicly available educational resources, peer-reviewed literature, and surgical guidelines. Questions are regularly reviewed and updated to reflect current standards of practice.

This app uses an adaptive learning algorithm that tracks your performance across individual concepts and prioritizes questions where you need the most practice. The AI assistant (PrepAI) is powered by a large language model and is intended as a supplementary learning aid—always verify AI-generated information with authoritative medical and surgical sources.

Medical Test Prep was founded by Mark P. Lachiewicz, MD, MPH, FACOG, CHSE, Associate Professor of Obstetrics and Gynecology and a minimally invasive gynecologic surgeon with more than 16 years of experience educating medical students, residents, fellows, and practicing physicians. Frustrated by the high cost of medical board preparation resources, he created Medical Test Prep with the goal of providing high-quality, affordable educational tools that help learners succeed.
