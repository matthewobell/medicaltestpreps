Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Medical Test Prep, LLC ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Medical Test Prep mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we may share or license it, and your choices regarding your information.

Please read this policy carefully. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Learning and Usage Data
When you use the App, we collect information about how you interact with educational features, including:

  • Quiz attempts, selected answers, scores, and performance metrics
  • Flashcard interactions, study session duration, and progress
  • Feature usage patterns and navigation behavior
  • Session-level activity, screen views, and engagement time
  • Specialty selection, exam type, and institution (where provided)

We store study information on your device. Depending on the features you use and your account setup, we may also store study information in Firebase, including quiz attempts, daily study time, and flashcards or questions you create. We collect session and usage information on our servers to operate, maintain, and improve the App.

1.2 Account Information (Optional)
If you choose to create an account, we collect your email address and any profile details you voluntarily provide, such as your name, institution, specialty, or graduation year. Account creation is optional, and many features are available without an account.

1.3 Support Communications
If you contact us for support, we collect your email address and the content of your message. This information is used solely to respond to your request.

1.4 Diagnostics and Stability Data
We may receive anonymized diagnostic or crash information through device or operating system settings to help improve performance and reliability. This data is not linked to your account.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and operate the core educational features of the App
  • Improve learning content quality, educational effectiveness, and App performance
  • Maintain App stability, security, and reliability
  • Respond to support requests and communicate with you about your account
  • Operate optional account features
  • Deliver sponsored or promotional content in accordance with Section 5
  • Generate aggregate, anonymized insights for research, benchmarking, and commercial data partnerships as described in Section 4

3. Data Linked to You

Learning activity, usage analytics, and some advertising records may be associated with an app session, device, or Firebase user ID. Firebase may assign a user ID even if you have not registered with an email address. If you sign in with a registered account, some information may be associated with that account.

We do not collect personal content outside the App, such as your contacts, photos, messages, or activity on other apps or websites.

4. Aggregate Data, Research, and Commercial Data Partnerships

We may use anonymized, aggregate data derived from App usage for research, benchmarking, and commercial data partnership purposes.

4.1 What "Aggregate, Anonymized Data" Means
Aggregate, anonymized data refers to information that has been de-identified and combined across many users so that no individual user can reasonably be identified.

4.2 How We May Use or License Aggregate Data
We may use or license aggregate, anonymized data for institutional benchmarking, medical device and pharmaceutical market research, academic and clinical research, and workforce and pipeline intelligence.

4.3 Individual Data Protections
We do not sell personal information. We do not provide sponsors, research partners, or data licensees with raw user-level data, individually attributable quiz scores, or individual study histories. We do not use individual learning performance to evaluate a physician’s clinical competency. We use service providers, including Google Firebase, to help operate the App as described in Section 6.

5. Sponsored and Promotional Content

The App is free to use. We offer a Premium account at no charge. The App may show paid sponsorships and other promotional content. Ads may be selected based on your platform, the exam or module you are studying, and where the ad appears in the App.

We may record whether a sponsored ad was displayed, whether you tapped its link, and whether you tapped Keep Learning. We use internal ad records, which may include a Firebase user ID and an ad display identifier, to match events to the correct display and prevent duplicate counts.

Sponsors receive campaign statistics, which may include totals by individual ad or platform. Sponsor reports exclude names, email addresses, Firebase user IDs, quiz answers, scores, and individual study histories.

A sponsor link may open a third-party website. Its own privacy policy governs the information it collects. Sponsored content does not influence quiz questions, answer keys, explanations, scoring, or other educational content in the App.

6. Analytics and Third-Party Services

We use Google Firebase for services including authentication, cloud storage, and usage analytics. Firebase Analytics helps us understand how people use the App. Some usage information may be associated with an app instance, device, session, or account as described in Section 3.

7. Data Retention

We retain account information for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and personal information as described in Section 10. Aggregate, anonymized data may be retained indefinitely.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for medical trainees and professionals and is not directed toward children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information we collect. No system is completely secure, and we encourage you to use a strong, unique password for your account.

10. Your Choices

  • Reset or delete local study data using in-app options in the Settings menu
  • Request deletion of your account and personal information using Settings → Delete my data → Request account deletion in the App, at medicaltestprep.org/deleteaccount.html, or by contacting us at admin@medicaltestprep.org
  • Uninstall the App to remove locally stored data from your device
  • Choose whether to create an account or use the App without signing in

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Your continued use of the App after any update constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
